
## 2026-05-20 - Auto-updating Dropdowns & Keyboard Submission
**Learning:** Users find it tedious to manually click 'Update' or 'Find' buttons after selecting from dropdowns or typing into a short text entry in Tkinter interfaces.
**Action:** Bind '<<ComboboxSelected>>' to dropdowns for immediate visual updates, and '<Return>' to text entries to allow quick, keyboard-only form submission without removing the existing buttons (which remain for discovery/mouse users).
