## 2024-07-01 - Combobox Auto-Update and Return Binding
**Learning:** Users expect dropdown menus (Comboboxes) to auto-update the content without needing to click a separate 'Update' or 'Show' button, and they expect to submit text forms (like Day Entry) by pressing Enter (`<Return>`).
**Action:** Bind `<<ComboboxSelected>>` to dropdowns and `<Return>` to entry fields in Tkinter to improve keyboard navigability and reduce click fatigue.
