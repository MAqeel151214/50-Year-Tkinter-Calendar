## 2024-05-23 - Auto-update on selection & Enter key support
**Learning:** Users naturally expect modern web-like UX patterns even in desktop apps; binding events like `<<ComboboxSelected>>` and `<Return>` reduces friction by removing the need to click "Update" buttons manually.
**Action:** Always bind Enter key `<Return>` on text inputs for form submissions and auto-update dropdown comboboxes when their selections change.
