## 2026-07-19 - Palette: Auto-update calendar and enter-to-submit
**Learning:** Modern web-like UX patterns such as binding `<<ComboboxSelected>>` to dropdowns and `<Return>` to text entries vastly improve the desktop GUI experience by saving clicks and improving navigation flow.
**Action:** Always bind action-triggering events to related form fields when appropriate rather than forcing the user to find and click a button.
