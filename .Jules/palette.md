## 2024-05-15 - Auto-update and Keyboard Shortcuts for Tkinter
**Learning:** Desktop applications feel much more responsive and modern when they adopt web-like interaction patterns (e.g., auto-updating on selection and submitting forms with Enter) rather than relying exclusively on explicit button clicks.
**Action:** Always bind `<<ComboboxSelected>>` to dropdowns and `<Return>` to text entries in Tkinter to reduce required clicks and improve keyboard accessibility.
