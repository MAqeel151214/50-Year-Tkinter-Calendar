## 2024-05-24 - Web-like interactions in Desktop Apps
**Learning:** Desktop Tkinter interfaces feel significantly more modern and intuitive when adopting web-like interaction patterns (e.g., auto-updating on dropdown selection or submitting on Enter key), reducing friction and unnecessary clicks.
**Action:** Always bind `<<ComboboxSelected>>` to auto-updating functions for Comboboxes and `<Return>` to submission functions for text entries.
